Game Pitch

Explore

Travel through Earth and nearby planets filled with hazards and secrets.

Collect

Find gems and resources needed to rebuild the damaged spaceship.

Repair

Turn collected materials into parts and work toward getting home.

Story

A Simple Mission

A lone alien crash lands on Earth after a ship malfunction. To return home, the player must travel across different planets, collect gems, and slowly repair the ship piece by piece.

Crash on Earth Collect gems Build ship parts Return home

Gameplay

What You Do

Jump & Move

Navigate platforms and dangerous terrain.

Use Teleporters

Travel from one world to another.

Avoid Hazards

Face icy gaps, lava zones, and unstable paths.

Progress Forward

Each new world gets you closer to repairing the ship.
