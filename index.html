Game Pitch

Crash Landing is a sci-fi exploration and survival game where the player controls an alien stranded far from home. After their spaceship malfunctions and crash lands on Earth, the player must explore new environments, collect gems, and use those gems to create ship parts.

The game is not only about reaching the end of a level. It is about slowly rebuilding the spaceship while discovering different planets, overcoming obstacles, and working toward the final goal of returning home.

Story

In Crash Landing, the player is an alien whose spaceship suddenly malfunctions and crash lands on Earth. The alien is far away from their home planet and cannot return until the ship is repaired.

To fix the spaceship, the player must collect gems from different planets. These gems can be brought back to the ship and turned into ship parts using a machine inside the spaceship. Each gem helps the player get closer to repairing the ship.

The alien also has a teleporter that can take them to other planets. However, the teleporter has limits. Since the alien’s home planet is extremely far from Earth, the player can only teleport to nearby planets first. This creates a journey where the player must travel step by step, collect resources, and slowly move closer to getting home.

Main Objective

The main objective is to find gems, turn them into ship parts, repair the spaceship, and eventually return to the alien’s home planet.

Gameplay

The player explores different areas while searching for gems. Gameplay can include walking, jumping, avoiding hazards, using a teleporter, and returning to the ship to upgrade or repair parts.

Each planet can introduce a different challenge. For example, Earth may act as the starting crash site, while another planet could have icy ground, dangerous terrain, or different obstacles that affects how the player should moves.

Explore

Travel through Earth and other nearby planets to search for gems and hidden areas.

Collect

Find gems that can be used as resources to create ship parts.

Repair

Use the ship machine to turn collected gems into parts needed to fix the spaceship.

Return Home

Continue upgrading the ship until the alien can finally travel back to their home planet.

Design and Illustrations

The design of Crash Landing follows a sci-fi adventure style. The game can include dark space colors, teleporters, and mysterious planet environments.

The art style should make the player feel like they are exploring unknown worlds. Each planet have its own look, such as an icy planet, or a strange uneven landscape.

Future Development

In the future, Crash Landing could develop into a larger adventure game with more planets, more story, and more detailed gameplay. The current playable version may only show the basic idea, but the full game could expand the world much further.

Future versions could include other different planets with unique mechanics. For example, unstable lighting where the player’s view changes during gameplay. Lights may flicker, fade, or turn off for a short time, making it harder to see obstacles, and the path before them. These new areas would make the game more challenging and give the player more reasons to keep exploring.

Future Features

  • More planets with different environments
  • Unstable lighting that affects the player’s view
  • Alien creatures or enemies to battle with
  • More types of gems and different usage
  • A clearer upgrade system inside the spaceship
  • More story scenes explaining the alien’s journey
  • A final ending scene where the player repairs the ship and returns home
